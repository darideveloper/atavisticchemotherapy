## Short version

This site sets no analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking cookies. No consent banner is needed because there is nothing to consent to.

## What is used

- The site is a static publication. Serving it relies only on the strictly necessary technical means of the web (page delivery by your browser). These do not identify you across sites and cannot be used for advertising.
- The contact form on the homepage sends the data you type to the trial team when you submit it. That transmission is covered by the [Privacy Policy](/privacy), not by cookies.

## Managing cookies in your browser

- You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. Doing so does not affect the reading of this site, since it does not depend on them.
