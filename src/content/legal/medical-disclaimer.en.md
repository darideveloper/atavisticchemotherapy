## Information only

- Everything on this site is general information. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it never replaces the judgment of a qualified oncologist who knows your case.

## Investigational treatment

- Atavistic Chemotherapy is an investigational approach. No outcome is promised or guaranteed for any individual, and no result described on this site predicts what will happen in your case.

## Past cases

- Patient stories and cases shown on this site reflect individual experiences. They appear with the documented consent of each patient and do not promise similar results for anyone else.

## Clinical trial reference

- The trial is identified as NCT02366884 on [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02366884). Consult that registry and your own physicians before making any decision.

## What to do

- Always consult a qualified health professional before starting, stopping, or changing any cancer treatment. If you face a medical emergency, seek immediate care.
