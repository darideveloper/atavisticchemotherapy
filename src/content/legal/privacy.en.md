## Data we collect

- The information you submit through the evaluation form: name, surname, email, phone, preferred contact day and time, and the medical context you describe.
- Nothing else is collected automatically. This site runs no analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking (see the [Cookie Policy](/cookies)).

## How we use it

- Your information is used solely to evaluate your case and respond with a free second opinion through your preferred channel (email, WhatsApp, or phone).
- Submissions are sent to and reviewed directly by the trial team (inbox-only). They are not stored in a database or customer-relationship system, and they are never sold or shared for marketing.

## Patient stories

- Names, diagnoses, photographs, and contact details shown in patient stories appear with the documented consent of each patient.

## Your rights

- You may request access, rectification, erasure, or objection regarding your personal data by writing to the contact channels above. Requests are handled under the applicable data-protection rules of the United States, Mexico, and the European Union.
