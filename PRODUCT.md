# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Women and couples seeking IVF/fertility care; secondary readers evaluating the specialist and indications before booking.

## Product Purpose

Aspire Centre for Women & Fertility landing page. Presents IVF care, specialist credibility (Dr. Fong Yang), indications, timing rationale, and a single-step IVF process overview. Success means the visitor books an appointment.

## Positioning

Personalised IVF care built around patient needs, backed by 28 years of O&G experience including male and female infertility — in Singapore private practice since 2002, ex-SGH/CGH consultant.

## Operating Context

Single marketing landing surface with six sections: sticky header nav (Who is IVF For? / IVF Cost / FAQ / About Dr Fong Yang), hero with appointment CTA, specialist bio, 4-card indication matrix, timing rationale with arch photo duo, consolidated process card, minimal footer. Contact via CONTACT US anchor and tel: link.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed sections: MainHeader, HeroSection, SpecialistProfileSection, WhoShouldConsiderSection, WhyActingNowSection, ProcessGuideSection, MainFooter. Anchors: #who-is-ivf-for, #ivf-cost, #faq, #about-dr-fong-yang, #contact, #appointment, #consultation. Explicitly out of scope for this record: content.md blueprint (ignored per user scope); pricing/FAQ detail beyond template anchors.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Aspire Centre for Women & Fertility Pte Ltd. Specialist: Dr. Fong Yang, MOH-accredited IVF doctor. Voice: reassuring, clinical-warm. Assets: design/code.html, design/screen.png are binding visual authority. External lh3.googleusercontent images are placeholders to replace on implement.

## Evidence on Hand

Template source: design/code.html (430 lines). Visual reference: design/screen.png. No real patient data, testimonials, or deployment claims in scope — do not fabricate.

## Product Principles

- Reassurance before detail: lead with outcomes, then explain.
- One page, one action: every section resolves to booking.
- Credibility is specific: names, accreditations, years — never generic claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Bilingual-capable audience (EN confirmed; ZH implied by media badges 联合早报 and language note). Target WCAG 2.2 AA on implement.
